Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who am I?
Hi, I'm Dave Gulliksen. I've been an anime enthusiast and collector since the 1980s when it was called "Japanimation." Over the years I've amassed a huge collection of anime VHS tapes, laser discs and DVDs, plus manga in English and Japanese and music on CDs. I live in the Myrtle Beach area of SC.
2. What am I selling?
What I'm currently selling on this page is my collection of about 450 anime CDs. I've sold my entire collection of laser discs, and these are the next most bulky items I have. I've been a seller on eBay for 20+ years, with a 100% satisfaction rating, account davegulliksen. I'm trying to price them at about 70-80% of the lowest price I can find online. It's a slow process to do the catalog entries and as of the beginning of March '26, I'm still tweaking this program, so please be patient.
3. Why am I selling it?
Now in my late 70s, it's time to think about what happens when I must move to much smaller living quarters like assisted living, which won't be able to house any of my collection. So I'm trying to get it into the hands of other collectors before the time comes when I have to dispose of it quickly.
4. Why not on eBay?
I'm using this page to sell them because I'm scanning and ripping them for my music storage in Foobar2000, and I can use the information in it to generate an updated selling list in a couple of minutes. eBay takes about an hour to enter each CD's information and scans, eBay bulk upload hasn't worked right, Discogs doesn't list a substantial number of my CDs, and neither does VGMdb. If you have wishes for a certain anime series or want to make an offer on a bulk purchase, click on any of the email or FaceBook buttons and leave me a message.
5. What form of payment can you use?
I currently accept PayPal, as shown at checkout, and may add options later.
6. How soon will it ship?
I generally ship within a few business days after payment is received. Include your email to get updates on what the tracking number is.
7. What is the return policy?
If there's a problem with your order, contact me at DaveGUrgent@aol.com and we'll work out a fair solution.
