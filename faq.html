Shipping & Policies FAQ

Everything you need to know before purchasing from the Japanese Anime CD Catalog.

What are the shipping rates?
$5 for the first CD, $1 for each additional CD in the same order. Free shipping when your order total before shipping is $50 or more. Outside the continental USA: please inquire for rates.
How are the CDs priced?
CDs are priced at approximately 70–80% of the lowest price found online — a great value for collectors.
Who is selling these CDs?
Dave Gulliksen, an anime enthusiast and collector since the 1980s, based in the Myrtle Beach, SC area. Dave has been an eBay seller for 20+ years with a 100% satisfaction rating (account: davegulliksen).
What payment methods are accepted?
PayPal is currently accepted. Additional options may be added in the future.
What is the return policy?
If there’s a problem with your order, contact Dave at DaveGUrgent@aol.com and a fair solution will be worked out.
How quickly will my order ship?
Generally within a few business days after payment is received. Include your email address to receive tracking number updates.
Can I make a bulk purchase or request a specific series?
Yes — use the Email or Facebook Messenger links on any CD page or on the main catalog to contact Dave directly.
