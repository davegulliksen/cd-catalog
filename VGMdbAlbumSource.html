Notes

01~15: Soundtrack for the first half of the OVA (Episode 1 to 6)
16~30: Soundtrack for the 3rd movie "Ranma Team vs Densetsu no Houou" (Released in North America as OVA)

Composition & Arrangement: Akihisa Matsuura (2~14), Kenji Kawai (16~29)

M-01: Opening Theme for OVA Episodes 1~6
Performed by YAWMIN
Lyrics & Composition: YAWMIN
Arrangement: Kitaroh Nakamura

M-15: Ending Theme for OVA Episodes 1~6
Performed by Ranma & Akane (Kappei Yamaguchi & Noriko Hidaka)
Lyrics: Ranma-teki Kagekidan Bungeibu
Composition: Kenji Kawai
Arrangement: Nobuyuki Nakamura

M-30: Opening Theme for Movie 3
Performed by DoCo
Lyrics: Ranmateki Kagekidan Bungeibu
Composition & Arrangement: Takeshi Yasuda

DoCo are
Megumi Hayashibara (Female Ranma)
Noriko Hidaka (Akane Tendo)
Rei Sakuma (Shampoo)
Minami Takayama (Nabiki Tendo)
